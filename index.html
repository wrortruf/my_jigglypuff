Dear Didi,

Kabhi kabhi sochta hoon... ki waqt kitni jaldi badal jaata hai. Kal tak hum bina kisi tension ke haste the, aur aaj har kisi ki apni alag zimmedariyaan hain. Lekin ek cheez kabhi nahi badli...

"Aapka mere liye hamesha hona."

Pehle pehle wo ek dusre ke strangers rehna, phir ek classmate banna, phir ab life ka sabse important hissa banna... Shayad tab humein ehsaas nahi tha, ki wahi pal ek din hamari sabse khoobsurat memories ban jaayenge.

Didi, Maine shayad kabhi itna express nahi kiya, lekin aapne hamesha mere liye jo bhi kiya... main uske liye dil se thankful hoon.

"Har daant ke peeche care thi... aur har smile ke peeche pyaar."

Life aaj pehle jaisi nahi rahi. Sab apni apni responsibilities mein busy ho gaye hain. Lekin meri life mein aapki jagah... kabhi nahi badlegi.

Main dil se dua karta hoon... Aap hamesha khush raho. Har sapna poora ho. Har musibat aapse door rahe. Aur aapki muskaan kabhi kam na ho.

"Aap mere sirf didi nahi... mere life ke ek important insaan bhi ho."

Is Rakshabandhan par mere paas koi mehenga gift nahi hai. Bas ye chhoti si app... aur usme likha hua mera dil.

Bas ek vaada... Jab bhi aapko meri zarurat hogi, main hamesha aapke saath khada rahunga.

❤️ Happy Rakshabandhan ❤️
